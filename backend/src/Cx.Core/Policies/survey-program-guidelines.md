# Survey Program Guidelines

## Survey invitations
The manufacturer sends a survey invitation by email to each new car customer after delivery. The customer answers the two survey questions online. Dealers do not send, edit, delete or resubmit survey responses.
Each customer receives one survey per purchase. A response cannot be changed by the dealer after it has been submitted.

## Timing
Invitations are sent shortly after delivery so the experience is still fresh. Dealers should make sure the customer's contact details in the sales system are correct and complete, because an invitation cannot be delivered to a wrong email address.

## What dealers must not do
Dealers must never coach customers on how to answer, ask customers to give a particular score, offer gifts or discounts in return for good scores, or complete a survey on behalf of a customer.
Attempts to manipulate survey results are a serious breach of the dealer agreement and may result in scores being excluded and further action being taken.

## Using survey results
Survey results are for improving the customer experience. Dealers should review their responses regularly, celebrate what customers value, and act on the low scores.
A recommended routine: review the dashboard weekly, review every survey with a net score below 50 in the weekly team meeting, and share the score and rank trend with the whole team monthly.

## Frequently asked questions
Why did my rank change when my score did not? Rank depends on all dealers' scores in the same period, so it moves when other dealers improve or decline.
Why does my score change when I change the date range? The score is the average of the surveys inside the selected dates, so a different range includes different surveys.
Why is there no score for the period? No surveys were submitted by customers in that date range, so there is nothing to average.
Can I see other dealers' scores? No. Dealers see their own data, plus the top ranked score as a benchmark. Only administrators see all dealers.
