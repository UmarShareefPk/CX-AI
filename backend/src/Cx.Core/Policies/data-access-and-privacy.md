# Data Access and Privacy

## Who can see what
A dealer user can see only the survey responses, customers and scores that belong to their own dealership. A dealer can never see another dealer's data.
The only cross-dealer information a dealer sees is the score of the top ranked dealer in the network, shown without the dealer's name, and the dealer's own rank out of the number of ranked dealers.
An administrator account can see the data of all dealers, the full leaderboard with dealer names, and the network wide score.

## Customer information
Customer names, email addresses and vehicle details are personal data. They are shown only to the dealer that sold the vehicle and to administrators, and only to support handling the survey response. Never copy customer information into emails, chat messages or other systems.

## AI assistant
The AI assistant answers questions using only the data the signed in user is allowed to see. It has no access to other dealers' data. If a question asks for another dealer's data, the assistant declines.
The assistant answers policy questions from the policy documents and quotes their titles. Its answers about scores and rank come directly from the platform data for the selected period, not from guesses. If the data is not available, the assistant says so instead of inventing numbers.
